Launch Site Database

A reference list of 49 places rockets are launched from, or are planned to be: orbital spaceports, suborbital and air-launch (horizontal) sites, and missile test ranges, with who runs each, when it first launched and its main pads. Filter the list, sort it by distance from any point, compare sites, and, if you choose, look up a site's current weather or the next launches scheduled there.

    How this is worked out

    The list. 49 sites, each with one position: a main launch pad where the site has one (the "Position marks" line says which), otherwise the facility's centre. Every position was checked to about 0.05° (about 5 km). Seven were moved because they were wrong: Cape Canaveral and Wallops to their pads, and Plesetsk (0.38° of longitude out), Vostochny, Taiyuan (the old position was Taiyuan city, about 140 km from the launch centre in Kelan County), Shahroud (the old position was the town) and Whalers Way (the old position was in the sea). Ten positions could not be confirmed to that accuracy and say so on the site: Yasny, Kapustin Yar, SaxaVord, Sutherland, Arnhem, Whalers Way, Woomera, Chabahar, Shahroud and Hammaguir (Andøya's position is its rocket range; the orbital pad nearby was not checked). "Country" is where the site is (Baikonur is in Kazakhstan, Kourou in French Guiana, Hammaguir in Algeria, San Marco off Kenya); "Operator" is who runs the site, not every company that launches from it. "First launch" is the first rocket launch there unless a note says otherwise (for example, first orbital launch). Status, operators and pads are as of mid-2025 and change: check a current source before relying on them.

    Distances use the haversine formula on a sphere of radius 6,371 km:

    a = sin²(Δφ/2) + cos φ₁ cos φ₂ sin²(Δλ/2)    d = 2R · atan2(√a, √(1 − a))

    with φ the latitudes and λ the longitudes. A sphere is within about 0.5 % of the distances on the WGS 84 ellipsoid, which is plenty for comparing sites.

    Upcoming launches come from Launch Library 2 by The Space Devs (ll.thespacedevs.com, API 2.2.0). Its free tier allows about 15 requests an hour from one address, so the page asks once per button press. Each launch is placed at the listed site nearest its pad, within 50 km; launches from elsewhere (at sea, or sites not in this list) are counted separately. The weather figure is the launch's published chance of favourable weather where Launch Library 2 lists one, not something this page works out. Current weather comes from Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com), a model analysis at the site's position, not a station report. Map tiles are from OpenStreetMap. Nothing else is fetched.

    Reference: Chris Veness, "Calculate distance, bearing and more between latitude/longitude points", movable-type.co.uk. Test: tests/launch-sites.test.js (Veness's Land's End to John o' Groats example, 968.9 km; Kennedy LC-39A to Cape Canaveral SLC-40, 5.79 km; filters, sorting and a sanity check of the list).
