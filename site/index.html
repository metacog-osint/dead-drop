Dead Drop Toolbox

38 standalone tools for open-source analysis of space, radio, geography and time. Each one runs in your browser and says how it works it out, with the reference it follows; nothing you type is sent anywhere unless a tool says it looks up public data and names the service.

Orbits and space

Delta-V Mission PlannerThe velocity change and propellant to move between two circular orbits, with an inclination change done the cheapest way. Eclipse Prediction CalculatorHow long a satellite in a circular orbit spends in the Earth's shadow each orbit, from its altitude and beta angle. Ground Track PlotterDraws the path on the ground beneath a satellite from its element set, split cleanly where it crosses the 180° meridian. Launch Azimuth CalculatorThe heading a rocket flies at liftoff to reach an orbit of a given inclination, with the correction for the Earth's rotation. Launch Vehicle Performance CalculatorA first estimate of a rocket's payload to a chosen orbit, scaled from its published payload to low Earth orbit, with the velocity changes worked out. Launch Window CalculatorThe times a launch site passes under a target orbit's plane, when a direct launch into that plane is possible. Multi-Satellite TrackerShows where several satellites are now on a map, with the ground track each has just flown and is about to fly, updated every few seconds. Orbital Decay EstimatorA rough estimate of how long atmospheric drag takes to bring a satellite down, with the spread solar activity puts on it. RF Satellite Tracking ToolPointing angles and Doppler shift for a satellite radio link: the frequency heard on the ground, the uplink frequency to transmit, and the Doppler through the next pass. Satellite Conjunction AnalysisScreens two satellites' element sets for close approaches in a time window: when they pass closest, how close, and how fast. Satellite Coverage PlannerWhen a satellite can see a ground target above a chosen elevation angle over the coming days, how much of the time that is, and the longest gap. Satellite Footprint CalculatorThe patch of ground a satellite can serve at or above a minimum elevation angle: its radius, area and edge range. Satellite Pass PredictorWhen a satellite next rises over a place, how high it gets, when it sets, and which passes could be seen with the eye. Satellite Visibility CalculatorWhere a satellite is in your sky at a given time, whether sunlight is on it, whether your sky is dark, and so whether you could see it with the eye. Slant Range CalculatorStraight-line distance from an observer to a satellite, from its altitude and elevation angle, with the signal delay. TLE AnalyzerReads a satellite's two-line element set field by field, checks it, and works out the orbit's period, size and height. Can look up current element sets from CelesTrak.

Launch

Flight Path AnalyzerGround track and end point of a flight from a launch point, azimuth and range, with the minimum-energy ballistic apogee, speed and flight time. Launch Site DatabaseA checked reference list of 49 orbital, suborbital and test launch sites worldwide, with a map, filters, distances and upcoming launches. Launch Weather DashboardScreens upcoming launches against forecast weather at the pad and current space weather, with indicative limits. A screening aid, not a launch decision.

RF and comms

Doppler Shift CalculatorFrequency shift of a radio signal from a moving transmitter, and the largest shift a satellite in a circular orbit can show a ground station. GNSS Interference IndicatorsA checklist of a satellite-navigation receiver's readings that can point to jamming or spoofing, against open-sky norms or the receiver's own normal. Ground Station Frequency MapA reference map of well-known satellite ground stations and the frequency bands their operators say they use, with a band look-up for any frequency. Link Budget CalculatorReceived power and link margin for a radio link over a clear line-of-sight path, with free-space loss per ITU-R P.525. RF Source TriangulationEstimate where a radio transmitter is from bearings or arrival-time differences at several receivers, with an error ellipse.

Geography and maps

Bearing and Distance OverlayDraw range rings and bearing lines on a map as true great-circle shapes, with each line's end point and arrival bearing. CSV to Map PlotterPlot many points on a map from CSV rows, with the great-circle distance and bearing of each leg in row order. Coordinate ConverterConvert latitude and longitude between decimal degrees, degrees-minutes-seconds and degrees-decimal-minutes, and to UTM and MGRS grid references. Distance and Bearing CalculatorGreat-circle distance, initial and final bearing, and midpoint between two latitude and longitude points. KML/KMZ VisualizerOpen a Google Earth KML or KMZ file on a map in your browser, with each line's length and each polygon's area, and copy it as GeoJSON. Light Pollution MapScout dark observing sites on a map with NASA's satellite image of night lights, keep a list of sites, and convert sky-brightness readings. Map ScreenshotSave a map view as a PNG or JPEG image with a title, notes, labelled markers, a scale bar and the map credit. Viewshed CalculatorWhether an observer can see each of a set of targets over the terrain between them, allowing for the Earth's curvature and refraction.

Images and files

Image Metadata ExtractorRead the EXIF metadata in a JPEG photo in your browser: GPS position, dates, camera and exposure settings. The image is never uploaded. Reverse Image GeocoderPut a photo on the map from the GPS position in its EXIF metadata, read in your browser. The photo is never uploaded.

Time

Multi-Time-Zone ConverterOne local time, in a named time zone, shown at launch sites, control centres and the common time zones, with daylight saving time and clock changes handled. Sun Position CalculatorWhere the Sun stands in the sky from any place at any moment, with sunrise, solar noon, sunset and twilight times, worked out in your browser. Time Zone Batch ConverterPaste many timestamps, one per line, in ISO 8601, Unix time, date-time groups or common written forms, and read each one in UTC and the time zones you choose.

Reference

Aerospace Unit ConverterConverts length, speed, mass, force, pressure, angle and temperature with exact NIST factors, plus Mach number and flight level.
